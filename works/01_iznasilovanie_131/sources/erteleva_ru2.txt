      Abstract. The article analyzes the attitude of the legislator to rape in foreign countries. The first stages of
  the formation of Ancient and medieval states in the aspect of the qualification of violent sexual acts are taken
  into account. Advanced legislative and law enforcement experience is noted, recommendations on borrowing a
  number of provisions within the framework of improving the Criminal Code of the Russian Federation are
  proposed.
